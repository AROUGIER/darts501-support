# Privacy Policy — Darts501

*Last updated: 15 September 2026.*

## In one sentence

**Nothing leaves your device.** The app has no account, no server, no analytics
and no advertising.

## What the app stores, and where

Everything is written to a local database on your device:

| Data | Why |
|---|---|
| Names and colours of the players you create | To tell players apart during a game |
| Games, legs, turns and darts | To keep score, undo, and resume |
| Impact positions on the board, when known | To compute accuracy statistics and heatmaps |
| Practice sessions and their scores | To track your progress |

This data is **never** transmitted. It goes away with the app: uninstalling
deletes everything.

## The camera

The current version of the app **does not use the camera** and never asks for
access to it. Darts are entered on the board drawn on screen, or with the
number pad.

Automatic dart detection with the camera is being explored. If it ships in a
future version, this page will describe what it does **before** it becomes
available.

## Children

The app collects nothing, so nothing concerning a child. It contains no in-app
purchases, no advertising, and no links out.

## Your rights

The GDPR grants rights of access, rectification and erasure over personal data
held by a controller. There is none here: nobody but you holds this data. In
practice —

- **access**: everything is visible in the app, under History and Statistics;
- **rectify**: scores can be corrected mid-game, players renamed;
- **erase**: deleting a profile removes the player without touching games
  already played, which keep their name; uninstalling the app deletes everything.

## If this changes

This page describes the app **as it is today**. Syncing between your devices via
iCloud is planned: the day it exists, your data will travel through **your**
iCloud account — so with Apple, under Apple's terms, and never with us. This page
will be updated before that happens, not after, and the app's privacy manifest
with it.
